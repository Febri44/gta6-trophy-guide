Platinum roadmap

About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). All single-player — no online trophies. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter (15 manual slots plus one autosave). Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them alone until the platinum pops.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story

    Chapters 1–5 and the story missions. Every chapter and mission trophy pops automatically. Do the one missable thing now: meditate with Lucia on the balcony at Jason's house in Chapter 1, the morning after “Down to Earth” — it is gone after Chapter 1.

    0 / 13 trophies

  2. Stage 2 — The 100% checklist

    After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Grind the counts: stunts, races, shooting ranges, cards, vehicles, areas, robberies and money. This is where A Long Road (100%) lives — the hardest trophy in the set.

    0 / 26 trophies

  3. Stage 3 — Strangers, side chains & clean-up

    Finish the 8 stranger strands, the five side-character finales (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), Bret's hunt, and the one-off misc trophies — bowling, the Ocean Beach cocktail, the showroom smash-out and the rest.
